## The need

Engineering teams work with drawings and documents that change by revision, status, discipline, and project. Without clear rules, it becomes difficult to identify the current document and follow its history.

## My contribution

I designed document control processes and procedures and collaborated on the Access-based implementation. The system model includes project/discipline/type classification, user roles, searchable records, revision history, and status tracking.

## Key capabilities

The supplied résumé describes dashboards for issued and pending documents, links to PDF/DWG/Excel files, activity/change logs, backups, and parsing of structured identifiers such as `E-PR-MNL-001`.

## Scope and evidence

This was collaborative work, not a claim that I independently built every module. Public overview: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aida-marvasti_microsoftaccess-vba-documentcontrol-activity-7493713745496526849-eHJg . Internal documents and user data are not published.
